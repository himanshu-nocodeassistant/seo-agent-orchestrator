## Your SEO Strategy

Replace this section with your own strategy. Consider including:

- **Current State**: Where your organic search is today
- **Target State**: Where you want to be in 6-12 months
- **Key Opportunities**: High-potential keywords or content gaps
- **Content Priorities**: What to create first and why
- **Technical Focus Areas**: Important technical SEO work
- **Link Building Strategy**: How you'll earn authority

### Tips for Your Strategy

1. **Be specific** — Generic strategies produce generic results
2. **Know your audience** — Write for the people who will hire/buy from you
3. **Focus on intent** — Match content to where people are in their journey
4. **Prioritize ruthlessly** — You can't do everything at once
5. **Track everything** — Measure so you know what's working
